About the project

A student project, reforged.

Created by AL2D as a modern successor to the original Companion Harmony Visualizer Tool, first developed as a university project in October 2023.

After moving into full-time software development and support, AL2D returned to the project to preserve its purpose and rebuild it with the experience gained since university.

AI disclosure: The visual design and frontend implementation of version 2 were produced entirely with AI under AL2D's direction and review. The original software, requirements, product decisions, and validation were supplied by AL2D.
