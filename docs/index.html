About the project

A student project, rebuilt.

Created by AL2D as a modern successor to the original Companion Harmony Visualizer Tool, first developed as a university project in October 2023.

After moving into full-time software development and support, I returned to preserve its purpose and rebuild it with the experience gained since university.

A note on AI: I used AI extensively for the frontend design, layout, and styling in v2. Frontend has never been my strong suit, so AI helped me fill that gap.
